# 11 — Experiment Protocol

The arc is an experiment with one subject. Treat it like one: fix the measures before you start, don't change them mid-flight without recording that you did, and pre-commit to what would count as failure.

**Deep Work Day Rate** — % of days with ≥ 1 completed deep block (≥ 40 min DSA or build).

## 3. Pre-registered success criteria

Write these down before you can rationalise them afterwards. Judged at Day 120.

**SUCCESS** — primary outcome met AND ≥ 6 of 8 career secondaries met AND enjoyment has not fallen in any domain.

**PARTIAL** — primary met, career secondaries 3–5 of 8. Interpretation: the system produced consistency but the consistency was pointed at the wrong things, or the targets were miscalibrated.

**FAILURE (system)** — primary outcome missed with core-quest completion below 50% for more than 4 weeks. Interpretation: the app failed at its job. The most likely cause is that it became the task (check the guardrail) or that the quests were unsustainable.

**FAILURE (measurement)** — the outcome numbers look good and you don't believe them. Check backdated-entry count and the XP-per-deep-hour ratio; this is the gaming signature.

**INFORMATIVE NULL** — you did the work, the process metrics are good, and the career outcomes didn't move. This is *not* a failure of the arc; it's evidence that 120 days of this particular work isn't sufficient for that outcome, which is genuinely useful information and is exactly the kind of answer a system that can't lie is built to produce.

## 4. Checkpoint protocol

15 minutes, same procedure every time, in this order. Order matters — enter data before seeing any analysis, so you're not anchored.

1. **Enter raw numbers first.** Weight, lifts, applications, interviews, screen time.
2. **Complete the three instruments** — self-efficacy (6 items), automaticity (4 items), enjoyment (3 items). Before looking at any chart.
3. **Read the auto-generated deltas.** Day 0 → now, and previous checkpoint → now.
4. **Rank gate evaluation.** Accept the result; it isn't negotiable.
5. **Read the verdict paragraph.** Rule-generated; it will name the bottleneck.
6. **Amend if needed.** The one legal window for changing core quests, targets, or if-then plans. All amendments are snapshotted.
7. **Export.** Mandatory — the checkpoint won't seal without one.
8. **Share** (optional but recommended) with your one named human.

## 5. Before / After report — Day 120

The deliverable of the whole experiment. Structure:

```
════════════════════════════════════════════
ARC REPORT · 1 SEP – 29 DEC 2026 · 120 DAYS
════════════════════════════════════════════

1. VERDICT
   One paragraph. Rule-generated. States plainly
   whether the primary outcome was met and what
   the evidence shows. No hedging.

2. REAL-WORLD CHANGE            ← leads. Level is a footnote.
   Career    │ Day 0 │ D30 │ D60 │ D90 │ D120 │ Δ
   Body      │ ...
   Lifestyle │ ...
   Psych     │ ...

3. WHAT THE DATA SAYS ABOUT HOW YOU WORK
   Best time of day for deep work, by completion rate
   Effect of sleep-window hits on next-day completion
   Effect of training on same-day deep work
   Your actual sustainable pace
   Which quests you skipped most, and when
   Topics that never reached Retained

4. WHAT DIDN'T WORK
   Quests below 50% completion
   If-then plans that stopped firing
   Rank gates missed, and which condition
   Targets that were wrong at Day 0

5. ENJOYMENT AND SUSTAINABILITY
   Enjoyment per domain across 5 checkpoints
   Automaticity per behaviour
   Guardrail: median app time across the arc
   Honest statement on whether this was sustainable

6. SYSTEM PROGRESSION            ← last, deliberately
   Level, XP, rank history, attributes, achievements

7. WHAT CONTINUES
   Two behaviours you nominate to continue UNMEASURED.
   One behaviour you stop entirely.
   The next arc, or the decision not to run one.
════════════════════════════════════════════
```

**Sections 2 and 4 lead. Section 6 — the entire game layer — comes last.** That ordering is the product's final statement about what it thinks matters.

**Section 7 is the countermeasure to the quantification risk.** You deliberately take two behaviours out of the measurement system to find out whether they survive without it. If they do, they were habits. If they don't, they were compliance — which is also worth knowing.

## 6. Rules for changing the system mid-arc

Necessary, because the temptation to tinker will be constant and tinkering invalidates the experiment.

**Allowed at any time:** logging anything, adding side quests, adjusting alarm times, pausing the arc, fixing an outright bug.

**Allowed only at checkpoints:** core quest definitions, XP values, targets, if-then plans, the quest set, rank gate thresholds.

**Never:** editing past events, un-sealing a checkpoint, retroactively changing XP rules to flatter a past period, deleting a bad week.

Every amendment writes an event and appears in the Day-120 report under "what changed and when." An experiment where the protocol changed silently is not an experiment.
